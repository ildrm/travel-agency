# Partner and agency local workflow manual

Use the theme's Partner portal link after signing in. A partner organization is a scoped record, not a WordPress administrator. Agency operators approve onboarding, assign active memberships and scope capabilities. A person belonging to several organizations must select the intended organization; changing a query parameter never grants access. Revocation blocks reads and writes immediately, including records the former member created.

Tour leaders: create a structured draft with stops, timed legs, days and private costs; submit for agency review; an independent authorized reviewer must approve before publication. Local times use named IANA zones. Costs and net rates are confidential; only approved public offer projections reach customers. Do not publish real supplier, schedule or insurance claims without evidence. Material changes to booked offers remain blocked until an approved impact/notification workflow exists.

Properties/rentals: local inventory is staff-only demonstration code, with resource ownership, dates/intervals, blackout/maintenance and return/inspection controls. It does not publish external availability or accept real deposit authorizations. A public request is not a reservation. No partner may override capacity or another organization's assets.

Makers: respond only to RFQs that name your organization; competitors' bids and artwork remain private. Agency selects a quote, approves a sample before production and records stages/inspection/invoice. A recorded manual payout approval/evidence is not an automatic bank transfer. Do not upload sensitive files through ordinary public media as a substitute for a reviewed private-file service.

Agency/support/finance: keep payment, booking confirmation, supplier issuance, refund and payout states separate. Verify local test receipts only as simulations; never use simulator outside local demo. On paid/released allocation, preserve money evidence and resolve exception/refund without reacquiring seats. Record reason and actual evidence; approvals and submitted refunds are not successful refunds. Read DOC-47 and implementation/OPERATIONAL-RUNBOOK.md for queue, backup and recovery.

These are development workflow instructions. Actual staff coverage, supplier agreements, human review, privacy/retention and user-facing terms must be approved before production. Current incomplete UI/advanced workflows remain listed in STATUS and coverage trace rather than implied by this manual.
