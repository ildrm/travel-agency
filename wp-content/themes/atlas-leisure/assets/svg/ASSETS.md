# Artwork provenance

`paper-landscape.svg` and the five `character-*-concept.svg` sheets are original geometric SVG sources authored in this workspace for Atlas Leisure. They do not depict actual facilities or destinations. These are early visual concepts, not approved brand assets or professionally cleared final production artwork. Character sheets show simple conceptual front/side/back views and are not finished production turnarounds. Human creative, cultural, trademark/similarity and accessibility review remains required. The theme is distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later; these included sources are provided under the same terms, subject to project owner confirmation of distribution policy. Decorative use should use empty alt text; meaningful use requires contextual text.
