# DOC-36 — Partner portals and contracts

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** govern organization membership, proposals, RFQs and contractual rights. **Prepared from perspectives:** partnerships, security, operations and finance. **Accountable reviewer:** partnerships lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-23](DOC-23_IDENTITY-RBAC-TENANCY.md), [DOC-27](DOC-27_SECURITY-PRIVACY-THREAT-MODEL.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md). **Decision:** one agency site, many partner organizations; no SaaS tenant or automatic bank split. MVP provides restricted leader/property/maker proposals; deeper procurement in phase 2.

## 1. Identity and agreement
**FR-PART-001:** ENT-ORG holds legal/display name, partner types, contact, review and suspension states; ENT-MEMBER associates a user to an organization with status, role and effective dates. A user may belong to several organizations; all resource access uses explicit organization context and object ownership, not only a global role. ENT-CONTRACT holds parties, version, effective period, approved services, commission basis, cancellation terms, signed evidence location and confidentiality flags. ENT-RFQ and ENT-QUOTE have recipient lists and independent approval/version/expiry. Verification/KYB requirements are determined by qualified reviewers, not assumed complete.

States: application draft → submitted → under review → approved/rejected → suspended/closed. Change of ownership or staff termination revokes memberships promptly and audits reason; suspension stops new proposals and transactions while preserving necessary historical records and support. Contract expiration blocks new commitments but does not erase past obligations. Org admins manage membership within their organization; agency staff approve partner and listing changes; partner users see only assigned owned records; finance sees only authorized costs and settlement; customers never see confidential supplier terms.

## 2. Workflow, screens and API
**WF-PART-001:** applicant enters organization and contact → uploads minimal requested evidence → agency performs recorded review → approves or requests changes → partner accepts proposed contract → scoped staff create a listing proposal → agency reviews and publishes. Manual RFQ in phase 2 selects recipients, captures quote deadlines, supports confidential responses and approval; no supplier commitment is inferred until signed purchase terms. **UI-PART-001** onboarding has clear draft/submitted/suspended states; **UI-PART-002** partner dashboard shows own proposals, assignments and permitted earnings with accessible tables. Notification: “Your proposal is under review” / «پیشنهاد شما در حال بررسی است».

**API-PART-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/organizations/{org_id}/proposals` checks logged-in identity, active membership, capability, ownership, CSRF protection where applicable and schema; return 201 proposal ID, 403 denied, 409 stale revision. A nonce is not authorization: WordPress says nonces are not authentication or access control ([official guidance](https://developer.wordpress.org/apis/security/nonces/), retrieved 2026-11-17). Auth checks apply equally to list, export, file download and background actions. Audit actor, organization, target, action, result and correlation ID; redact secrets. Retention and breach process follow DOC-27.

## 3. Fictional example and exceptions
A guide organization with two staff submits a two-stop tour; agency requests a route clarification, approves revision 2 and publishes a dated departure. Guide A edits within organization; guide B can view but not submit. Their manager leaves, is removed and immediately loses access. A confidential supplier net rate stays hidden from customers and unrelated partners. No commercial agreement is implied by this example.

| Exception | Control and operational response |
|---|---|
| Partner suspended | Block new commitments, notify agency and affected customers where necessary; retain past bookings and arrange service/refund. |
| Staff changes organization | Revoke membership and sessions/permissions as appropriate; recheck object access on each request. |
| Approval race | Conditional version check yields conflict; refresh and require reviewer decision on latest version. |
| Expired contract | Prevent new commitment; notify owner before/after expiry and preserve existing obligations. |
| Fraud or disputed quote | Restrict relevant actions, create case, preserve evidence with access controls; no automatic adverse action without policy. |
| Cross-organization identifier guessed | Return generic denial, log authorization event; no private data or rate leakage. |

## 4. Tests and decisions
**TC-AUTH-001** test every read/write/export/file with two organizations and multi-membership; **TC-PART-001** removal immediately blocks resource access; **TC-PART-002** suspended partner cannot create new offers; **TC-PART-003** stale approval fails. Test retained bookings, disclosure of private cost, upload policy, RTL names and keyboard tables. Aggregate onboarding completion, time to review, proposal rejection reason categories and disputes without sensitive identity. **OPEN DECISION:** partnerships/legal define KYB evidence, delegation, contract approval, suspension appeal, ownership transfers and retention; finance approves commission policy. No verified partner or contract exists.

## 5. Contract and workflow details
Agency reviewers record which organization and legal entity is party to a contract, effective dates, services, territories, permitted marketing claims, settlement cycle, termination, disclosure and dispute handling. A proposed commission can be fixed or percentage based, but its tax and calculation base must be explicitly specified and reviewed before use. Contract acceptance, approval and record version are separate; the absence of an approved contract does not erase historic bookings but blocks new commitments. Invitations have expiry and are accepted by an authenticated named user; an invitation does not itself grant access to an organization until accepted and activated. An organization manager cannot grant a capability they do not have. Supplier selection on an RFQ uses an explicit recipient list; an amendment increments version and sends all invited recipients the same material change. Agency staff record a reason if bids are withdrawn or a supplier is disqualified. Do not put private quote amounts in public catalog, search metadata, notification previews or analytics. Data portability and access removal are governed by DOC-23 and DOC-27; partner feedback is factual, access-controlled and subject to an appeal process approved by operations.
