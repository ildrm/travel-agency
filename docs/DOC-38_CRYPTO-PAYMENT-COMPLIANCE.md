# DOC-38 — Digital-asset payment compliance investigation

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** specify a dormant investigation boundary and safeguards, without promising activation. **Prepared from perspectives:** legal, treasury, security and payment operations. **Accountable reviewer:** qualified legal counsel with finance controller. **Dependencies:** [DOC-37](DOC-37_PAYMENTS-DOMESTIC-GATEWAYS.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md), [DOC-43](DOC-43_LEGAL-REGULATORY-REVIEW.md). **Decision:** FR-CRYPTO-001 is OFF and unavailable in every phase; no enabled checkout option, processor, asset, network, jurisdiction or launch date is approved.

## 1. Guardrails and approval evidence
REGULATORY REVIEW REQUIRED: do not infer that any domestic or cross-border use is lawful or unlawful from this draft. Qualified counsel must determine current rules in all relevant jurisdictions, sanctions, consumer rights, tax, AML/KYC, data and licensing obligations; finance must assess accounting, risk, custody, processor eligibility, refunds and valuation. Product owner must accept risk and security must review architecture. Until documented, specific approvals and an implementable compliant policy exist, the feature remains absent from payment selection, API offers and configuration that could activate it. A simple admin toggle is insufficient authorization.

A future approval record would identify jurisdiction, merchant entity, legal opinion/date/expiry, asset and network, processor agreement, controls, responsible owner, review cadence, permitted customer/payment types and revocation mechanism. The approved asset/network matrix is **empty**. An investigation should first establish lawful eligibility and relevant contracts; do not route transactions, circumvent restrictions or collect blockchain credentials. No supplier capability is verified. If review declines approval, close investigation and preserve audit; no funds should be solicited.

## 2. Dormant conceptual model
**WF-CRYPTO-001** is research only: request → legal and treasury assessment → explicit risk decision → security and implementation review → separate build proposal. There is no production payment workflow. An illustrative risk register could record wrong network/asset, underpayment, overpayment, late transfer, reorganization, lost processor access, valuation changes and refund mismatch. Any actual recovery or handling policy must be approved by counsel and contracted provider; do not assert that funds can be recovered or define network workarounds.

**UI-CRYPTO-001:** no method appears to customers; authorized staff see only “Not available; investigation requires approval” / «در دسترس نیست؛ بررسی نیازمند تأیید است». **API-CRYPTO-001:** any proposed availability response returns disabled and does not generate addresses, invoices or instructions; unsupported payment requests fail closed and are logged without personal wallet data. Plugin absence, configuration changes, rollback and multilingual display must not accidentally expose the method. No invented external endpoint or live transaction is part of this document.

| Hypothetical incident | Investigation-only policy |
|---|---|
| Wrong network or asset | No address is issued by this product; if a report occurs, route to legal/finance and contracted provider only, without promising recovery. |
| Under/overpayment | Do not mark paid; finance applies a separately approved policy only after eligibility established. |
| Delayed or reorganized confirmation | Do not confirm booking on unverified event; provider and finality policies require review. |
| Value differs at refund | No automatic conversion or refund promise; counsel and treasury must define terms before any feature work. |
| Sanctioned/ineligible jurisdiction | Deny availability; compliance review owns any subsequent determination. |

## 3. Verification and decisions
**TC-CRYPTO-001:** checkout in all locales and product types has no digital-asset method; **TC-CRYPTO-002:** direct crafted request cannot enable, generate payment details or change payment state; **TC-CRYPTO-003:** plugin upgrades or restored settings retain off state; **TC-CRYPTO-004:** no private wallet details in logs. **OPEN DECISION:** counsel and treasury must determine whether research should continue at all, then document all prerequisites; no approval or date exists. Track investigation status without customer transaction analytics.

## 4. Investigation control and verification
The compliance owner maintains an evidence checklist, not a list of currencies enabled by configuration. It records the legal opinion's scope and date, all relevant jurisdictions, documented processor eligibility, merchant identity, consumer disclosures, risk ownership, security review and a planned reassessment date. A changed law, sanctions status, processor relationship or business model triggers a fresh review; an expired or withdrawn opinion cannot be treated as continued permission. If a future design is ever proposed, it must address truthful payment states, refunds, financial reporting, data retention, customer support and effective disabling under failure before implementation is authorized. None of that implies these functions should be created now. Test that stale browser sessions, direct API access, old saved carts and restored backups cannot reveal a method that was never approved. A support agent who receives an unsolicited payment report uses the general incident workflow, records minimal facts and escalates to finance and counsel rather than offering an unverified technical recovery. No marketing materials should advertise an unavailable payment method.
