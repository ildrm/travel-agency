# DOC-31 — Academy courses and cohorts

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** define scheduled in-person learning and admission. **Prepared from perspectives:** academy operations, instructors, accessibility and booking. **Accountable reviewer:** Academy director. **Dependencies:** [DOC-24](DOC-24_BOOKING-INVENTORY-REGISTRATION.md), [DOC-30](DOC-30_EVENTS-MANAGEMENT.md), [DOC-27](DOC-27_SECURITY-PRIVACY-THREAT-MODEL.md). **Decision:** MVP offers fixed-price cohorts and basic attendance; assessment and certificates are phase 2, conditional on academic policy.

## 1. Domain and workflow
**FR-ACA-001:** ENT-COURSE is a reusable syllabus with localized outcomes, prerequisites and version. ENT-COHORT is a scheduled delivery with city/venue, instructor assignments, session instants and IANA zones, registration window, capacity, tuition, accessibility and policies. ENT-ENROLLMENT binds one learner and cohort with eligibility evidence, hold, booking, attendance and completion status; a payer may differ from learner. ENT-CERTIFICATE has opaque verification reference, version, issuer, issue/revocation status, never an easily enumerable public student record. Materials remain accessible to authorized enrolled learners after admission closes under the published access policy.

**WF-ACA-001:** coordinator proposes course and cohort → agency approves → visibility/public admission → eligibility review → atomic hold → payment verified → manual confirmation → attendance → phase-2 assessment → completion → conditional certificate issuance. Each cohort independently uses WF-REG-001 in DOC-30: open inclusive, close exclusive, TTL min(10 minutes, time remaining), payment deadline equals hold expiry, and independent verification completed locally at or before that deadline may consume a still-allocated hold without provider `paid_at`. After the deadline, trustworthy provider `paid_at` at or before the deadline and a still-unreleased allocation are both required. If released, booking transitions `expired → exception` with prior expiry retained in audit; allocation remains released and payment remains verified. Route to manual refund review without new admission or automatic reclaim. A post-close exception requires authorized staff, reason and capacity check. No certificate implies accreditation unless verified.

**UI-ACA-001** course page lists cohorts and each independent state; **UI-ACA-002** learner form collects identity and only necessary prerequisites/guardian details; **UI-ACA-003** instructor roster and attendance; phase-2 reviewer enters assessment and certificate decision. **API-ACA-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/cohorts/{id}/enrollments` validates the authenticated purchaser/learner relationship and applicable operator organization membership, prerequisites, cohort and an existing accepted hold in a transaction. Capacity is allocated only by the shared hold transaction; enrollment references that hold and never allocates a second seat. Retry is idempotent. Instructor sees only assigned cohorts and minimum roster; agency reviewer approves; learner sees own result. Prevent instructor or room overlapping sessions by interval checks, with an explicit reviewed exception. Store evidence securely and apply retention policy; aggregate funnel and attendance metrics only.

## 2. Fictional scenario B — Professional Tour Leadership
Course v1 requires adult applicants and introductory guiding experience. Cohort A Tehran: 20 seats, 12,000,000 IRR, registration 2027-05-01 09:00 to 2027-05-20 18:00 Asia/Tehran, sessions 2027-06-01 and 06-02. Cohort B Shiraz: 25 seats, 14,000,000 IRR, registration 2027-06-01 09:00 to 2027-06-20 18:00 Asia/Tehran, sessions 2027-07-01 and 07-02. At 2027-06-05 A is closed while B is open. Prices and dates fictional. Applicant supplies name, eligibility statement and contact, selects B, reads terms, signs in and obtains a hold; payment verifies and Academy coordinator confirms. Instructor records both sessions' attendance. Phase 2 example requires both attendance and an independently assessed practical exercise; an approved reviewer issues a revocable certificate. Verification returns only status, issue date and course name with learner-consented disclosure. Price is for tuition only; venue/instructor availability requires manual confirmation, not a supplier API. Confirmation: “Your enrollment awaits confirmation” / «ثبت‌نام شما در انتظار تأیید است».

| Failure | Detection and action |
|---|---|
| A closed, B open | Each cohort is checked against own UTC interval; reject A with clear message, B remains available, no charge for A. |
| Last seat contested | Transaction locks cohort capacity; exactly one hold wins; loser offered permitted waitlist. |
| Prerequisite missing | Show required evidence, reject admission until reviewed; no charge, retain minimum information. |
| Instructor double-booked | Flag schedule conflict at proposal and publication; block until reassignment or auditable exception. |
| Verified payment after a released hold | Transition booking `expired → exception` with prior expiry audited; allocation remains released and payment remains verified. Route to manual refund review without confirmed enrollment or new admission. |
| Failed assessment or absence | Record result privately; explain retake path approved by Academy, no automatic certificate. |
| Certificate revoked or verification reference guessed | Public endpoint rate-limited; revoked returns status without personal details, log issuer action. |

## 3. Acceptance and decisions
**TC-ACA-001** simultaneously render A closed/B open and reject closed server-side; **TC-REG-001** exact open/close and time-zone rules; **TC-ACA-002** no certificate when required attendance or assessment absent; **TC-AUTH-001** instructor from another organization cannot read roster. Test edits to schedules, waitlist after close, payment races, accessible venue information and materials after cutoff. Notifications: cohort changes to enrolled learner and assigned instructor; sensitive assessment only via authenticated portal. **OPEN DECISION:** director to approve prerequisite evidence, attendance thresholds, assessment appeals, certificate content, verification disclosure and material retention; instructor, venue and legal contracts not evidenced.

## 4. Curriculum and completion controls
The course record has syllabus version, objective, duration, languages and prerequisite description. Cohort record independently fixes the syllabus version, local venue, timezone, session sequence, instructor assignment, tuition and published accessibility information. Editing a course does not silently rewrite the syllabus previously agreed by enrolled learners. An instructor may mark attendance only on an assigned session, and a correction records actor, reason, original value and time. If a learner transfers cohorts, operations checks destination eligibility and capacity and records price difference and consent; it does not move a paid enrollment automatically. In phase 2, assessment records rubric version, reviewer, attempt, score or pass decision, date and appeal status. Only a specifically authorized issuer can create or revoke a certificate; revocation produces a reason and notification subject to policy. Certificates and results are not public simply because a verifier exists. At each screen display separate course and cohort labels and announce cutoff using the cohort's named zone. A learner who completed a cohort retains confirmation and materials for the published, approved access period even after registrations close.
