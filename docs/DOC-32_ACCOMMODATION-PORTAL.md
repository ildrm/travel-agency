# DOC-32 — Accommodation portal

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** define property onboarding, stay quotes and future bookable room-night inventory. **Prepared from perspectives:** lodging distribution, partner operations, booking and finance. **Accountable reviewer:** accommodation operations lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-24](DOC-24_BOOKING-INVENTORY-REGISTRATION.md), [DOC-36](DOC-36_PARTNER-PORTALS-CONTRACTS.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md). **Decision:** MVP properties submit proposals and customers request quotes only; no payment, availability promise or live booking. Operational stays are phase 2; a verified channel connection is phase 3.

## 1. Data, state and authorization
**FR-STAY-001:** an ENT-PROPERTY belongs to a partner organization and includes localized address, zone, description, facilities, access constraints and review evidence. ENT-UNITTYPE includes occupancy, capacity, amenities; ENT-UNIT identifies an individually blocked unit or villa. ENT-RATE defines period, eligible occupancy, meal plan, currency, taxes, refundable terms, price and expiry. ENT-INVENTORY counts local room-type inventory per night, while individually unique villas use overlapping-interval exclusion. ENT-QUOTE stores requested dates, guests, price components, expiry, supplier approval and version. Supplier net rate and rooming list are restricted. Local date is property zone; checkout date is exclusive.

**WF-STAY-001:** property submits proposal → agency reviews → publishes factual listing → customer requests quote → supplier and agency verify inventory/terms → agency communicates offer. MVP ends at request/quote: booking is manual offline if separately arranged and not represented as confirmed on this platform. In phase 2, only after contracts and tests, reserve every requested night atomically in one local transaction, with a unique inventory lock order to prevent deadlocks; rollback all nights if any unavailable. External inventory and local locks cannot share a transaction; recheck with supplier, reconcile and compensate via outbox. Booking confirmation is separate from payment and supplier acceptance. Holds and late payment follow DOC-24.

**UI-STAY-001** property and date search shows freshness and “Request a quote” / «درخواست قیمت» for MVP; **UI-STAY-002** partner form captures room types, policy, rates and accessible features. **API-STAY-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/stay-requests` accepts property, local check-in/out, occupancy, contact and idempotency key; validates checkout > check-in, date range, ownership and consent, returning request ID, not availability. Partner reads only owned property and assigned requests; customer reads own; agency approves changes. Store rooming data only when needed and limit access; audit exports and retention. Aggregate requests, quotes and confirmed stays without names in analytics.

## 2. Fictional scenario D — Hotel and entire villa
A 20-room hotel has 12 standard and 8 family rooms; a separate villa is a single book-the-entire-property unit. Requested nights: 2027-06-10, 11 and 12 (checkout June 13). Fictional nightly standard refundable quote is 8,000,000 IRR; nonrefundable is 7,000,000; family 12,000,000; villa 30,000,000. One standard room for three nights is quoted at 24,000,000 refundable or 21,000,000 nonrefundable before explicitly stated tax; group asks for four rooms and an agency quote. Supplier approval, room allocation and expiry must be obtained manually. MVP customer submits request; agency checks hotel and sends a non-binding quote. Phase 2 illustrative outcome after contracts: customer chooses refundable plan and hold, pays, and receives `paid_pending_confirmation`; supplier accepts, agency marks booking confirmed. Price, payment and supplier states are distinct. No current supplier capability implied.

| Edge | Detection and outcome |
|---|---|
| Overlap across any night | Transaction validates all nights; any unavailable night rolls back whole hold, shows alternatives. |
| Last room race | Competing holds serialize and exactly one succeeds; losing customer is not charged. |
| Villa overlap | Interval overlap blocks allocation even for a different room-type label; notify operator. |
| Stale supplier data | Show request-only, refresh supplier quote, no instant promise; log freshness. |
| Payment succeeds but supplier rejects | Booking exception; notify support/customer, offer consented alternative or approved refund, reconcile ledger. |
| Nonrefundable plan selected | Show policy before purchase; approved exceptions handled by support, do not silently switch rate. |
| Group request exceeds allocation | Create RFQ rather than partial confirmed inventory; disclose response timing and private pricing. |

## 3. Tests and decisions
**TC-STAY-001** overlapping three-night capacity and atomic rollback; **TC-STAY-002** unique villa collision; **TC-STAY-003** payment/supplier failure reconciliation; **TC-AUTH-001** partner cannot read another property's rooming list. Include daylight-saving transitions, rate expiry, policy changes after purchase and accessible accommodation accuracy. **OPEN DECISION:** operator to establish merchant role, cancellation windows, allotment rights, no-show, tax and accommodation compliance; supplier agreements and retention review are prerequisites for phase 2. No provider endpoint or live property inventory is asserted.

## 4. Rate plan and operational controls
Property staff propose rate-plan name, unit type, occupancy, local start/end dates, minimum/maximum nights, meal inclusion, taxes, cancellation cutoff, amount and currency; agency approval is required before publication. For the future nightly allocator, a change to room count or a maintenance block must check already allocated nights and reject a reduction that would conflict. Rate changes create a new version, preserving previous quote and booking terms. For a group request, record number of units by type, occupancy, guest distribution and quotation expiry; do not reveal individual traveler data to a property until needed and authorized. When a supplier confirms a phase-2 reservation, capture who confirmed, when, permitted supplier reference and applicable conditions. If channel data later disagrees with local allocations, flag an inventory exception and stop further sales on affected dates until staff reconcile; do not blindly overwrite commitments. A canceled stay triggers separate release, supplier communication and refund review. Inventory age, availability source and manual-confirmation status are visible to support; detailed rooming lists are never indexed publicly. Accessibility statements require evidence from the property, and guests can request assistance without disclosing health details in public fields.
