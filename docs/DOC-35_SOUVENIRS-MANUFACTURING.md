# DOC-35 — Souvenirs and manufacturing

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** cover ordinary Woo physical products and future custom production. **Prepared from perspectives:** merchandising, procurement, logistics and finance. **Accountable reviewer:** merchandise operations lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-25](DOC-25_WOOCOMMERCE-COMMERCE-ENGINE.md), [DOC-36](DOC-36_PARTNER-PORTALS-CONTRACTS.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md). **Decision:** MVP supports Woo physical souvenirs, with shipping and maker proposals; phase 2 adds RFQs and production. Mixed physical/service carts are blocked in MVP.

## 1. Model and interaction
**FR-MERCH-001:** ENT-MERCH is a Woo product/variation, with SKU, localized description, dimensions, weight, tax/shipping class, stock, price and return terms. ENT-RFQ stores buyer, specifications, quantity, delivery deadline, permitted recipients, version and due date; ENT-QUOTE stores supplier unit price, production lead time, setup, shipping, tax and expiry. ENT-PRODUCTION stores selected supplier, approved sample version, milestones, quality checks, shipment and invoice references; confidential artwork is access-controlled and scan-checked. Define intellectual-property permission and retention before production.

**WF-MERCH-001:** agency publishes product → customer signs in and buys physical goods in a physical-only cart → verified payment → pick/pack/ship → customer support. Supplier manufacturing path in phase 2: agency creates RFQ → suppliers submit private bids → agency compares → selects supplier → reviews sample → authorizes production → inspects → receives invoice → finance records payable and manually settles after approval. Quote is not a purchase order; production cannot begin merely because RFQ is sent. Woo order, production, shipment, refund and payout are independent. Use Woo APIs for product and order access; do not assume vendor marketplace or automatic payment splitting.

**UI-MERCH-001** product page shows variants, total, shipping policy and accurate stock. **UI-MERCH-002** private RFQ form includes artwork version, dimensions, material, quantity, destination, due date, proof and inspection requirements. **API-MERCH-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/rfqs` accepts scoped suppliers, deadline and requirements; returns draft ID; submitting creates audit and notifications. Organization membership gates bids and files; a maker cannot see competitors’ bids. Downloads are authorized per object, filenames sanitized and content scanned; no public art URLs by default. Analytics tracks aggregate conversion, late production and defect rates.

## 2. Fictional scenario G — 500 conference gifts
Agency requests 500 custom welcome gifts with a logo, recycled packaging, sample approval, delivery 2027-10-15. Supplier A quotes 500 × 600,000 IRR = 300,000,000 plus 10,000,000 setup; B quotes 325,000,000 including setup; C quotes 290,000,000 plus 30,000,000 shipping. Tax terms are to be separately confirmed. Figures are fictional and are not supplier offers. Agency compares lead time and quality, selects A under its proposed terms, approves a physical sample and records art v3, places an authorized order, monitors production, receives shipment, inspects 500 items, and authorizes invoice after exceptions are resolved. In this fictional sequence, finance then records a completed manual payout of 310,000,000 IRR, attaches illustrative bank evidence and reconciles it to the approved invoice and ledger; no real transfer or payment evidence exists. Supplier payout is separate from the customer order. “Sample approval required” / «تأیید نمونه الزامی است» appears before production; only agency and selected supplier see approved artwork and price. Notifications cover RFQ, question, quote deadline, selection, sample, shipment and inspection.

| Failure | Detection and operation |
|---|---|
| Supplier misses deadline | Mark quote late, notify agency; no automatic award or liability. |
| Artwork has no rights or unsafe file | Quarantine and block sharing/production pending authorized replacement. |
| Sample differs from proof | Reject with comments, preserve version, do not authorize production. |
| Supplier changes price after selection | Require revised quote and agency acceptance; retain earlier snapshot. |
| Quantity short or defects found | Record inspection, dispute invoice, agree remedy before payment. |
| Shipping delayed or lost | Notify agency and customer where applicable, track manual claim and revised fulfillment. |
| Unauthorized competitor access | Deny scoped request and audit; no bid or private artwork disclosure. |

## 3. Tests and decisions
**TC-MERCH-001** physical-only cart checks shipping and refuses service item; **TC-MERCH-002** another maker cannot see bids/art; **TC-MERCH-003** no production authorization until sample approved; **TC-MERCH-004** failed inspection blocks payable approval. Test stock races, variants, refunds, file expiry, return exceptions and multilingual product data. **OPEN DECISION:** procurement owner must approve IP, order cancellation, minimum quantities, quality tolerance, shipping terms and payout evidence. Supplier availability and extension compatibility remain unverified.

## 4. Fulfillment and product control
An agency merchant controls Woo product publication, permitted variations, physical stock, shipping zones, packaging and return policy. A customer-facing product must distinguish available stock from an item made to order; no custom lead time is represented as a guaranteed delivery date without an accepted supplier commitment. Woo remains authoritative for cart and order totals; maker proposals become sales products only after agency review. Manufacturer proposals may include materials, capacity, samples, certifications claimed with evidence, minimum order quantity and payment terms; each statement remains unverified until reviewed. A production order preserves the winning quote, approved artwork hash/version, delivery and quality requirements, quantities, agreed price and written authorization. Changing the artwork after approval creates a new review cycle. Inspection records quantity received, accepted, rejected, defect description, date and reviewer; short shipments are not marked complete. Shipping status uses verified carrier evidence when available and an honest manual state otherwise. Buyer returns and manufacturer defects are separate workflows, each governed by their own approved terms and financial treatment.
