# DOC-30 — Events management

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** specify event occurrences and enforce registration. **Prepared from perspectives:** event operations, inventory, UX and finance. **Accountable reviewer:** event operations lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-24](DOC-24_BOOKING-INVENTORY-REGISTRATION.md), [DOC-37](DOC-37_PAYMENTS-DOMESTIC-GATEWAYS.md). **Decision:** MVP supports manual fixed-capacity events and confirmation; sophisticated session seating and sponsors are later.

## 1. Model, rights and rules
**FR-EVT-001:** a reusable ENT-EVENT describes localized program, eligibility, terms and venue; each ENT-OCCURRENCE is independently scheduled and stores UTC instants plus named IANA zone, visibility start/end, registration open and close, capacity, tiers, price/tax snapshot, payment deadline policy, sessions, optional meal/transport, waiver version and waitlist policy. Validate open < close ≤ event start unless an agency exception is approved; unpublished drafts never accept public registration. Distinguish visible-not-open, open, sold-out, closed, suspended, canceled and rescheduled. Sessions have independent capacity when enabled; no overselling event or session inventory.

**WF-REG-001:** ordinary admission occurs atomically at hold creation only for `open_at <= server_now < close_at`, eligibility and capacity. Hold TTL = min(10 minutes, close minus now); payment deadline is hold expiry. Independent payment verification completed locally at or before the deadline may consume a still-allocated hold without provider `paid_at`. After the deadline, require trustworthy provider `paid_at` at or before the deadline and a still-unreleased allocation. If the hold was released, booking transitions `expired → exception` with prior expiry retained in audit; allocation remains released and payment remains verified. Route to manual refund review with no new admission or automatic reclaim. If trustworthy payment time is unavailable after deadline, manual exception. An authorized admin may grant an explicit post-close exception with reason, actor and audit; capacity still applies. Waitlist invitations never override closure. Booking, order, payment and event status remain distinct.

**UI-EVT-001** occurrence detail exposes zone, dates, price, availability, age, venue accessibility, program, gear, waiver, optional services and clear registration state. **UI-EVT-002** registration asks purchaser, attendee, guardian if needed, dietary options, transport and agreement. Only necessary dietary details are retained with restricted access and defined retention. **API-BOOK-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/holds` accepts offer ID/version, optional tier ID, quantity, participant eligibility references and an idempotency key. The server resolves the occurrence from the selected offer; the client does not submit a second authoritative occurrence ID. The response includes hold ID, expiry and price snapshot, or 409 for cutoff/capacity. Server time and atomic inventory rule govern, regardless of UI state. Partner may propose owned event; agency reviews; assigned check-in staff can see only needed attendee list and mark attendance, not payment instruments. Notification and audit events cover publication, hold, payment, confirmation, change and cancellation.

## 3. Tests, operations and decisions
**TC-REG-001:** open−1ms fails, open succeeds, close−1ms succeeds only if hold can be created, close fails; verify TTL and UTC conversion. **TC-REG-002:** parallel attempts on final seat admit one. **TC-EVT-001:** locally verified on-time payment needs no provider timestamp; after-deadline verification requires trusted on-time provider timestamp and unreleased allocation, and released hold yields booking `expired → exception`, prior expiry audited, allocation released and payment verified without new admission; **TC-EVT-002:** session and event capacities both enforced. Test duplicate callbacks, waitlist exception, attendee list authorization, dietary data redaction, reduced motion and mixed scripts. KPI aggregates: published-to-view, view-to-hold, hold-to-paid, no-show and refund rates; do not send sensitive fields to analytics. **OPEN DECISION:** owner must set waitlist, cancellation and guardian rules; venue contracts and qualified local legal review remain dependencies. Accept only after a production-like time/race test and operator signoff.

## 4. Administrative configuration and change control
Event operators configure publication window, zone, capacity, waitlist policy, age eligibility, accessibility information, cancellation terms and attendance staff for each occurrence. Publication is rejected if open/close is missing or reversed; warning text identifies the field and remains available to assistive technology. A capacity reduction cannot reduce allocated seats silently: block the change, show the allocated count and require an operational resolution. A canceled or rescheduled occurrence suspends admission, records old and new schedules, approval actor, reason and affected booking IDs. Existing customers receive a versioned notice and any refund or transfer is a separately approved action. In a proposed session-based event, a registration must secure both occurrence and session quotas atomically; if an option sells out, rollback the entire requested allocation and offer a different selection, never a partial surprise. Check-in records attendance, operator and time; offline check-in synchronization must resolve duplicate scans without exposing attendee details on public displays. Payment, cancellation, attendance and check-in reporting are separate aggregate measures.
