# DOC-39 — Finance, pricing and settlements

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** define accurate commerce snapshots and controlled manual reconciliation. **Prepared from perspectives:** finance, accounting, payments and operations. **Accountable reviewer:** finance controller. **Dependencies:** [DOC-25](DOC-25_WOOCOMMERCE-COMMERCE-ENGINE.md), [DOC-37](DOC-37_PAYMENTS-DOMESTIC-GATEWAYS.md), [DOC-36](DOC-36_PARTNER-PORTALS-CONTRACTS.md). **Decision:** proposed MVP single agency merchant of record, integer IRR, no automatic partner bank split; contracts and local legal/tax review remain open (ADR-007/008).

## 1. Pricing and records
**FR-FIN-001:** Woo totals are authoritative order snapshots; ENT-QUOTE stores offering/version, quantity, unit price, fee, discount, tax, total, currency, expiration and policy versions. Use integer IRR without floating point; explicit display conversion is 10 IRR per toman, with no ambiguous currency labels. Provider amount conversion is separately configured and tested from verified provider docs. Define approved rounding for any non-divisible display values and never lose the original integer amount. A changed or expired quote requires explicit customer acceptance of a new price; completed order snapshots are immutable except through auditable adjustment/refund records. Taxes, legal invoice format and jurisdiction-specific rates require qualified review rather than assumed rates.

ENT-LEDGER records signed amount, currency, event type, references to order/payment/booking/partner, recognition period and operator, with correcting entries instead of silent edits. Payment verification, revenue recognition, supplier payable, refund and settlement are distinct; ledger is an operational proposal, not asserted statutory accounting. ENT-REFUND records policy, requested/approved amount, reason, provider reference and `requested → approved → submitted → succeeded/failed`; partial refund never automatically cancels unrelated booking or payment. Payouts require authorized approval and independent evidence of transfer; no assumed split gateway or automated disbursement.

## 2. Workflow, interfaces and exceptions
**WF-FIN-001:** customer accepts quote → order total snapshot → verified payment → operational ledger entry → supplier service confirmation → calculate contractual payable → human review → manual settlement → reconcile provider statement, order, payment, booking, refund and bank evidence. Reconciliation flags unmatched, duplicate, unit mismatch, unknown and partial records. Rows retain evidence reference, exception owner, reason, next action and audit; two-person approval threshold is an open decision. Never include secrets or full payment credentials. Export access is limited and audited; retention is set by counsel and finance.

**UI-FIN-001** finance dashboard separates paid, pending confirmation, refund pending and payable; displays gross, deductions and outstanding balance with currency and provenance. **API-FIN-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/refunds/{id}/approve` requires finance capability, object authorization, state/version match and idempotency key; returns approved state, not proof provider paid. Partner sees only its own approved contract and payable statements, not other organizations' data. Marketing analytics excludes traveler identities and financial account details. Accessibility requires labeled amounts, error context and logical RTL/LTR number ordering.

## 3. Fictional ledger example
One event seat: Woo total 10,000,000 IRR (1,000,000 toman). Independently verified payment 10,000,000; proposed supplier contractual share 6,000,000; agency operational gross retained 4,000,000 before tax, gateway fees and other costs. These amounts are invented; not a legal revenue-recognition conclusion. If service is canceled and full refund approved, issue a separate refund for 10,000,000, then adjust/reverse supplier payable under its actual contract. If provider refund is submitted but not verified, show pending and reconcile; never record bank settlement from a button click alone. Notices: “Refund is being processed” / «بازپرداخت در حال پردازش است».

| Exception | Handling |
|---|---|
| Provider statement differs from order | Flag unmatched amount and currency, preserve evidence, assign finance owner; hold payout. |
| Gateway and Woo use different units | Compare configured exact units, reject mismatch, correct only with reviewed evidence. |
| Verified payment but supplier fails | Maintain verified payment; support resolves or initiates independent refund, reconcile supplier liability. |
| Partial refund | Reference original transaction, cap cumulative approved amount per policy, recompute remaining entitlement and audit. |
| Chargeback | Record separate dispute and deadline, notify support and finance; do not silently delete payment or booking. |
| Partner dispute or suspended account | Freeze disputed payout, preserve contract and evidence, record resolution; no unrelated account leakage. |
| Exchange rate expires | Future foreign quote is requoted and accepted; no stale conversion or hidden FX fee. |

## 4. Tests and decisions
**TC-FIN-001** integer IRR and toman comparison with provider conversion; **TC-FIN-002** duplicate settlement evidence does not create duplicate payout; **TC-FIN-003** partial refunds cannot exceed permitted original amount; **TC-FIN-004** payment, booking and payable reconcile independently; **TC-AUTH-001** finance export and partner statements are scoped. Test negative adjustments, reversal, rounding, month close, untrusted provider timestamp and retry. Metrics are aggregate reconciliation lag and exception age; targets are proposed in DOC-42, not measured. **OPEN DECISION:** finance/counsel determine merchant, tax, recognition, contracts, refund authority, payout timing, currency and retention before launch.

## 5. Reconciliation controls and evidence
For each accounting period, finance imports or records authoritative evidence from the selected provider and bank using approved handling procedures. Match by verified merchant and transaction reference, currency, integer amount and direction; fuzzy matches remain manual candidates, never automatic proof. Compare payment, Woo order, booking, refund and supplier liability independently and preserve the origin of every amount. A duplicate payment is not an extra entitlement: identify the duplicate, verify provider facts, contact the customer and route refund or other resolution through approved policy. A supplier invoice is matched to an approved contract, delivered service, authorized price and any prior payment before finance approves the payable. Settlement status becomes complete only when acceptable bank evidence is recorded; bank instructions require separate access and change audit. The close procedure lists unresolved exceptions, their owners and aging, and explicitly records whether the period is conditionally closed. Finance decides accounting treatment and statutory reporting; this system's operational events are not a substitute for professional accounting. Retain exact IRR snapshots even when an interface rounds a toman display.
