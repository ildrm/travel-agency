# DOC-34 — Transfers and rentals

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** distinguish chauffeured transport from self-drive vehicle rental. **Prepared from perspectives:** fleet operations, safety, partner and finance. **Accountable reviewer:** ground transport lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-24](DOC-24_BOOKING-INVENTORY-REGISTRATION.md), [DOC-36](DOC-36_PARTNER-PORTALS-CONTRACTS.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md). **Decision:** MVP is RFQ only, with no collection or live availability promise; phase 2 may support contracted booking after safety and insurance review.

## 1. Fields, state and permissions
**FR-XFER-001:** transfer inquiry includes pickup/drop-off location and zoned time, passengers, luggage, accessibility, vehicle class and flight reference optional. Dispatcher assigns an eligible vehicle and driver only after agency review; waiting fees, cancellation and tracking require explicit terms and verified technical/legal basis. **FR-RENT-001:** ENT-VEHICLE records organization, category (bicycle, motorcycle, car, van), availability intervals, capacity, license class, insurance status and maintenance block; ENT-RENTAL records local pickup/drop-off instants, renter, required driver eligibility, quote, deposit terms, fuel/mileage policy and signed condition records. Avoid retaining images or licenses longer than approved policy; never ask for raw payment card data.

**WF-RENT-001:** request → agency/supplier quote → customer agrees → (phase 2) interval hold and payment where contracted → manual confirmation → identity/license check → pickup inspection → return inspection → settle approved fees/deposit → close or dispute. Prevent overlapping rental, transfer and maintenance intervals for the same vehicle; acquire ordered locks, validate interval `[start,end)` and keep buffer for inspection. Deposit authorization versus actual charge is provider-specific and unverified; do not represent an authorization as captured or promise it exists. No automatic payout. **UI-RENT-001** quote request displays inclusions, coverage exclusions, required documents and “Request only” / «فقط درخواست قیمت»; UI-RENT-002 pickup/return checklist supports photos subject to consent and access. **API-RENT-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/rental-requests` stores dates, vehicle class and contact, returning request ID. Partner sees own vehicles and assigned request; renter sees own record; finance sees monetary details; admin approval is required for publication and material changes.

### Transfer request interface and contract
**UI-XFER-001** is a dedicated chauffeured-transfer request form using the accessible RFQ pattern. It collects pickup and drop-off locations and local date/time with named IANA zone, passenger and luggage counts, vehicle class, contact information, accessibility needs and optional flight reference. It explains that an agency will seek a driver; it shows no driver assignment, live dispatch, tracking or payment in MVP. A requester may submit an inquiry under public RFQ abuse controls; subsequent request access requires verified identity and ownership. Agency staff and an explicitly assigned operator have scoped access; other partners do not.

**API-XFER-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/transfer-requests` accepts those fields and an idempotency key and returns an unconfirmed request ID, never a booking or availability guarantee. Server validation requires bounded nonempty locations and contact, integer passengers greater than zero, a nonnegative luggage count, an allowed vehicle class, valid local instants and recognized IANA zones. Convert each local time using its stated zone, reject nonexistent or ambiguous local times without explicit resolution, and require drop-off after pickup. Reject malformed or conflicting idempotency retries; apply rate limits and abuse controls without revealing other requests. No provider endpoint, payment, live dispatch or tracking capability is assumed.

## 2. Fictional scenario E — four-day car rental
Customer requests a car 2027-09-01 10:00 to 2027-09-05 10:00 Asia/Tehran. Quote is 4 × 5,000,000 IRR = 20,000,000, optional protection 2,000,000, potential refundable deposit 10,000,000; full-to-full fuel and late rate 500,000 per hour, tax separately disclosed. These are illustrative and not an offer. Provider manually confirms eligibility and terms; in MVP this is an RFQ without platform payment or confirmed inventory. Future phase 2 contracted path: verify license, reserve interval, collect verified agreed rent, confirm provider, inspect with renter and condition evidence; return at 11:00 yields proposed one-hour late fee, subject to agreed terms and customer dispute process. Deposit instrument handling is contingent on verified provider support. Chauffeured airport transfer instead quotes a driver, pickup time, passenger/luggage count and waiting terms; renter license and self-drive deposit do not apply. Notifications use “Your request is under review” / «درخواست شما در حال بررسی است» until confirmed.

| Exception | Detection, recovery and finance |
|---|---|
| License expired/ineligible | Deny pickup, communicate requirements privately; any fee/refund follows signed terms. |
| Vehicle unavailable or overlapping | Lock interval and block conflicting booking; offer alternate only with consent. |
| Deposit authorization unavailable | Do not claim deposit secured; pause confirmation, offer verified approved option. |
| Pickup no-show | Record timestamp and contact attempts; apply reviewed terms, notify customer and finance. |
| Late return | Record actual return, show calculation and dispute route; no unapproved charge. |
| Damage disagreement | Preserve both condition records, restrict access, create dispute and suspend disputed settlement pending review. |
| Driver absent for transfer | Dispatch replacement if contracted or refund under terms; notify traveler, no false tracking. |

## 3. Tests and decisions
**TC-RENT-001** interval collision including maintenance and buffer; **TC-RENT-002** expired license prevents handover; **TC-RENT-003** damage dispute does not automatically charge; **TC-XFER-001** transfer uses driver assignment, not renter license rules. **TC-XFER-002** submit a valid request and verify the response contains an unconfirmed request ID, not a booking; reject invalid time zones, reversed instants, nonpositive passengers and conflicting idempotency retries, enforce abuse limits and deny access to another owner. Test tax, daylight saving, missed pickup, device-offline checklists and unauthorized partner access. Aggregate inquiry-to-quote, rental utilization and dispute counts without location trails. **OPEN DECISION:** operations/legal must approve coverage, licensing, security deposit, provider capabilities, fee disputes, safety escalation and document retention. No live tracking is assumed.

## 4. Operational screens and configuration
Agency staff configure permitted vehicle categories, service zones, required license categories, buffers, pricing components, pickup rules, insurance documents and approved disclosure versions. The supplier portal supports a vehicle proposal with make/model or category, passenger capacity, registration reference, inspection expiry and out-of-service interval. Sensitive identifiers are visible only to staff with a service need. A later allocation engine must reject conflicts in both rental and transfer schedules, including turnaround buffers; prevent a car that is already assigned to a chauffeured journey from appearing available for self-drive. At pickup, record actual fuel, mileage, existing damage and signatures with time and actor; at return, retain both parties' versions if disputed. Do not infer consent from an unchecked protection option or charge a deposit without a documented payment method. Manual fallback records attempts to contact supplier, replacement offer, explicit customer acceptance and any refund decision. The transfer interface asks for pickup location and time before quoting and displays waiting fee and cancellation policy rather than treating a transfer as a car rental.
