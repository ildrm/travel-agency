# DOC-40 — Support, notifications and CRM

**Status:** DRAFT specification; review pending. **Purpose:** connect customers, partners and operations through controlled cases and reliable notices. **Prepared from perspectives:** support, operations, privacy and service design. **Accountable reviewer:** customer support lead. **Dependencies:** [DOC-27](DOC-27_SECURITY-PRIVACY-THREAT-MODEL.md), [DOC-28](DOC-28_INFRA-DEVOPS-RELIABILITY.md), [DOC-39](DOC-39_FINANCE-PRICING-SETTLEMENTS.md). **Decision:** MVP uses agency-operated support and transactional notifications; provider connectivity and SLA remain unverified.

## 1. Case data and workflow
**FR-SUP-001:** ENT-SUPPORT has case ID, category, customer or partner organization, related booking/order, priority, owner, state, timestamps, limited notes, next action and audit; ENT-NOTIFICATION has event reference, recipient, approved template/language, channel, attempt and delivery state, with no payment secret. Case states: new → triaged → waiting_customer / waiting_supplier / in_progress → resolved → closed, with reopen and escalation. Resolution and refund/payment completion are independent. Distinguish transactional notices required for service from optional marketing; use consent/preferences and jurisdictional review. Emergency communication policy and actual staffing/coverage require approval.

**WF-SUP-001:** customer opens case → identity and ownership checked → triage priority → assign authorized operator → request supplier action or finance review → communicate next step → resolve and close with reason. Emergency safety incidents follow a separately approved human escalation tree, not an automated guarantee. Disruption messages use verified facts, intended recipients, approved localized template and accessible channel. Outbox delivers transactional notices after committed state; retries are idempotent, with bounded retry and dead-letter review. A notification failure cannot roll back payment or imply that a notice was delivered. Avoid storing passport, card, health or location information in general notes; restrict necessary attachments and apply retention.

**UI-SUP-001** customer case list shows status, next action and secure reply. **UI-SUP-002** operator queue groups by urgency and exception age, with permission-scoped booking, notification and audit history. **API-SUP-001** proposed `POST /agency/v1/support-cases` validates authenticated owner, optional related object and request length; response 201 case ID, generic 403 for unauthorized object; idempotency prevents duplicate creation. Partner only sees own cases; support sees assigned cases and minimum contextual data; finance sees refund case details only when authorized. Staff do not impersonate users without approved, audited procedure.

## 2. Fictional payment and trip disruption cases
A traveler paid 10,000,000 IRR for an event, but hold was released before verification. Booking transitions `expired → exception` with prior expiry retained in audit; allocation remains released and payment remains verified. An exception case opens automatically with masked reference for manual refund review, with no new admission. Agent tells customer “We received your payment and are reviewing the reservation” / «پرداخت شما دریافت شد و رزرو در حال بررسی است». Finance reviews an approved refund; agent states refund pending until provider evidence confirms it. Notification retry uses event ID and recipient key so duplicate callback cannot send a misleading duplicate. Separately, a tour leg is delayed: operator records known change, identifies affected confirmed travelers, and sends an approved bilingual notice with contact channel; do not promise alternative transport until supplier confirms it. Escalation ownership and actual response hours are proposals until staffed.

| Exception | Detection and recovery |
|---|---|
| Email/SMS provider unavailable | Retry boundedly, mark failed and queue operator alternative; no claim of delivery. |
| Incorrect recipient or translation | Stop pending sends, record incident, correct and reissue after privacy review; audit access. |
| Duplicate callback/event | Deduplicate by event/template/recipient; update case once. |
| Traveler reports emergency | Route according to approved escalation tree and region, record times and owner; never imply 24/7 coverage without staffing. |
| Supplier disputes booking | Keep case open, notify customer of status, obtain evidence and route payment/refund to finance. |
| Customer changes contact details | Verify identity, update with audit, show notice and avoid leaking prior communications. |
| Account deletion with open obligations | Apply reviewed retention/legal hold and restricted access; do not erase necessary case evidence blindly. |

## 3. Tests and decisions
**TC-SUP-001** case owner cannot read another customer’s case; **TC-SUP-002** outbox replay yields one logical notice; **TC-SUP-003** failed delivery creates operator task without changing payment; **TC-SUP-004** agent cannot claim refund completed before authoritative confirmation. Test RTL templates, bidi identifiers, screen-reader status announcements, inaccessible contact channels, attachment access and notification opt-out distinction. Aggregate resolution time, backlog age, notice delivery failure and satisfaction only under approved privacy policy; no invented SLA performance. **OPEN DECISION:** support owner must set operating hours, channel providers, escalation contacts, templates, retention, severity matrix and actual response targets; privacy/legal review determines consent and emergency handling.

## 4. Message templates, escalation and permissions
Each approved template stores event type, version, language, channel, required variables, fallback behavior, owner, approval date and whether it is transactional or optional marketing. Rendering validates variables and escapes text for the output channel. Payment notices never call an order confirmed solely because a provider redirected the browser; ticket notices require issuance evidence. When delivery is unknown, the queue shows unknown rather than delivered; retries follow provider-specific verified capabilities and do not send to a superseded contact without appropriate checks. Support staff can see only cases and operational facts needed for assignment; viewing identity documents, issuing refunds and changing payment configuration require separate capabilities. A priority matrix is proposed: immediate safety concern to a staffed human escalation channel; paid-but-unfulfilled or departure-imminent to operations and finance; routine inquiries to normal queue. Owners must set actual hours and maximum response targets before publishing any promise. Capture resolution code, action owner and timestamps, not sensitive free-text details in analytics. An inaccessible email or phone channel must have an approved alternative, and Persian and English templates receive human review before production.
